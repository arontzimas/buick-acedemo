    Buick Envision FAQs

    How much is a Buick Envision?

    The 2026 Buick Envision starts at an MSRP of $41,300*.  It comes in three trims: Preferred,  Sport Touring and Avenir.

     

    The 2026 Buick Envision offers three trim levels — Preferred, Sport Touring and Avenir — each priced and equipped to meet different priorities around design, comfort and technology.

    • Preferred (MSRP starting at $41,300): Standard features include the ultrawide 30" diagonal touch screen, Google built-in*, a Bose* 9-speaker premium audio system, wireless smartphone charging*, a Head-Up Display* and 18" machined alloy wheels.
    • Sport Touring (MSRP starting at $43,800*): Features 20" Carbon Flash Metallic wheels, gloss black grille accents, leather-wrapped flat-bottom steering wheel and ST-embossed front-seat head restraints. The interior offers perforated leather-appointed seats with leatherette inserts and includes sporty upgrades like alloy sport pedals.
    • Avenir (MSRP starting at $50,700*): Offers high-end features like a panoramic moonroof, quilted heated and ventilated leather-appointed front seats, a massaging driver seat, heated outboard rear seats, and hands-free programmable power liftgate with LED logo projection. Avenir also includes 20" Pearl Nickel wheels, dual-zone automatic climate control and a Universal Home Remote.

     

    Each trim level of the 2025 Envision includes standard all-wheel drive and advanced safety and driver assistance technologies*.

     

    Get more information on the 2025 Envision.  

    What is the Buick Envision towing capacity?

    The Buick Envision can tow up to 1,500 lbs.* across all trim levels when properly equipped.

     

    That 1,500-lb. towing capacity applies to every trim of the Envision, because they share the same 2.0L turbocharged engine and 9-speed automatic transmission. 

     

    While 1,500 lbs.* of towing capability isn’t enough to haul heavy campers or large boats, it’s perfect for small utility trailers, ATVs, jet skis or bike/dirt bike trailers.

     

    When towing a trailer:

    • Follow all state and local laws that apply to trailer towing. These requirements vary from state to state.
    • Install extended side view mirrors on your vehicle if your visibility is limited or restricted while towing. State laws may require the use of extended side view mirrors.
    • Do not tow a trailer during the first 800 km (500 mi) of vehicle use to prevent damage to the vehicle.
    • Perform the first oil change before heavy towing.
    • Do not drive over 80 km/h (50 mph) and do not make starts at full acceleration during the first 800 km (500 mi) of trailer towing.
    • Tow in D (Drive). If equipped, Tow/Haul mode is recommended for heavier trailers. If the transmission downshifts too often, a lower gear may be selected using Manual mode.

     

    For more information about Envision towing capability, check out the Owner’s Manual.  

    How long is a Buick Envision?

    The 2026 Buick Envision measures 182.7 inches long or just over 15 feet.

     

    Envision is a compact SUV that strikes a balance between interior space and maneuverability. 

     

    That size offers several practical advantages:

    • It gives you good cabin volume for five passengers without overly stretching the wheelbase.
    • It fits more easily into tighter parking spaces, urban garages and crowded lots than full-size SUVs.
    • It offers excellent visibility and control in city driving or when navigating narrow streets.

     

    Envision is roomy enough for daily needs and weekend gear but compact enough to handle everyday driving.

    What is a Buick Envision?

    The Buick Envision is a compact crossover SUV (sometimes called a compact SUV) that blends utility, comfort and premium features in a size tailored for everyday driving.

     

    Envision offers a balance of versatility and efficiency without the bulk of a full-size SUV. It delivers available all-wheel drive and cargo flexibility and plenty of technology features.  The Envision also offers a premium experience with features like QuietTuning® with Active Noise Cancellation, a sweeping 30" diagonal LCD screen display and advanced safety and driver assistance features*. 

     

    Because it blends agility with upscale features, the Envision works especially well for commuting, family errands and even longer highway trips, providing impressive comfort, style and versatility.

    Is the Buick Envision all-wheel drive?

    Yes — the Buick Envision comes standard with intelligent all-wheel drive, featuring an active twin clutch system to automatically manage traction.

     

    Every 2026 Envision is delivered with standard all-wheel drive (AWD) on all trims. The intelligent all-wheel drive with active twin clutch monitors driving conditions and transfers power between wheels as needed to optimize grip and handling. 

     

    This standard AWD setup helps give buyers built-in confidence in changing road conditions — whether it’s rain, snow or winding back roads.

    What engine is in the Buick Envision?

    The Buick Envision is powered by a 2.0L Turbo 4-cylinder engine which delivers 228 horsepower and 258 lb.-ft. of torque.

     

    The 2.0L Turbo 4-cylinder engine is standard on all Envision trims. This turbocharged engine boasts 228 horsepower and 258 lb.-ft. of torque, making it well suited to everyday driving demands as well as highway merging and passing.

     

    The Envision’s engine also features engine stop/start technology, which helps improve fuel efficiency* in stop-and-go traffic.

    How many seats does a Buick Envision have?

    The Buick Envision is a two-row SUV and seats five passengers comfortably.

     

    The Envision is designed with a two-row seating configuration that comfortably seats up to five.

     

    This layout helps keep the vehicle’s overall footprint more compact and manageable, helping to make it easier to maneuver in urban settings and park in tighter spots. At the same time, Envision offers generous front- and rear-seat legroom.

     

    That said, for buyers needing more passenger capacity or expecting to carry more people regularly, the Buick Enclave is a three-row SUV that seats up to seven.

    What are the different models of the Buick Envision?

    The Buick Envision is offered in three trims: Preferred, Sport Touring and Avenir, each with its own character.

     

    The three Envision trims allow buyers to choose the model that fits their needs:

    • Preferred offers standard all-wheel drive, comfort and tech features like a 30-inch diagonal ultrawide display, adaptive headlights, heated mirrors and a 9-speaker Bose* audio system.
    • Sport Touring adds athletic flair with 20-inch Carbon Flash Metallic wheels, black exterior accents, a flat-bottom leather-wrapped steering wheel and sportier interior touches.
    • Avenir includes premium quilted leather-appointed upholstery, ventilated and heated front seats, heated outboard rear seats, a panoramic moonroof, hands-free liftgate with LED logo projection and more upscale interior finishes.

     

    Compare Trims to review your choice of options.

    Is the Buick Envision bigger than the Buick Encore GX?

    Yes, the Buick Envision is larger than the Buick Encore GX in length, width and height.

     

    Here’s how they compare: The 2025 Envision measures 182.7 inches long, 74.1 inches wide and 64.6 inches tall. By contrast, the 2025 Encore GX is about 171.2 inches long, 71.4 inches wide and 64.1 inches tall.

     

    Envision offers generous interior space, especially rear-seat legroom and cargo behind the rear seats. That extra size is well suited for buyers who regularly carry up to five passengers, carry gear or take longer road trips.

    What gas does the Buick Envision take?

    The Buick Envision uses regular unleaded gasoline with an octane rating of 87 or higher.

     

    Drivers sometimes wonder whether a turbocharged engine like the Envision’s requires premium fuel. In this case, the engine is calibrated to run efficiently on 87-octane fuel, so you won’t gain significant performance or efficiency by routinely using higher-octane gas.

     

    For full details, such as any notes about ethanol blends, fuel additives or fueling tips, consult the “Fuel” section of the vehicle Owner’s Manual.

    Can you put a trailer hitch on a Buick Envision?

    Yes, a trailer hitch can be installed on a Buick Envision, provided the hitch is properly rated and installed in accordance with the Owner’s Manual.

     

    Installing a trailer hitch on Envision is a common upgrade. Many aftermarket hitches bolt directly to the vehicle with no welding required. However, adding a hitch (should be installed by an authorized installer or by a Buick/GM dealer) doesn’t change the vehicle’s factory towing limits — so it’s crucial to consult the towing section of the Owner’s Manual before towing.

     

    Once you have a properly rated hitch installed, this opens up a lot of possibilities:

    • You can attach a bike rack, small cargo carrier or platform for extra gear without loading the roof.
    • You can tow lightweight trailers, including utility trailers, jet skis or compact campers.
    • You can use it for tow-behind devices like small lawn equipment or hobby trailers.

     

    When setting up towing, pay close attention to these details:

    • Use the correct electrical wiring harness so trailer lights, turn signals and brakes function properly.
    • Respect tongue weight limits along with gross trailer weight limits; even a light trailer can cause handling issues if tongue weight is too high or too low.
    • Distribute cargo carefully and recheck payload, so you don’t exceed the vehicle’s limits.
    • Periodically inspect the hitch, bolts and wiring for wear, corrosion or loosening.

     

    For more details on Envision towing, visit page 224 of the Owner’s Manual.
