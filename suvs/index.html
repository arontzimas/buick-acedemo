What safety and driver assistance features are available in Buick SUVs?

The 2025 Envista and 2025 Encore GX come standard with Buick Driver Confidence, a suite of advanced safety and driver-assistance features, which includes Automatic Emergency Braking, Forward Collision Alert, IntelliBeam Auto High Beams, Following Distance Indicator, Front Pedestrian Braking and Lane Keep Assist with Lane Departure Warning.

 

The 2025 Enclave and 2025 Envision come standard with a suite of safety and driver assistance technologies, which includes HD Surround Vision, Intersection Automatic Emergency Braking, Forward Collision Alert, Blind Zone Steering Assist, Enhanced Automatic Emergency Braking, Reverse Automatic Braking, Front Pedestrian and Bicyclist Braking, Lane Keep Assist with Lane Departure Warning, Following Distance Indicator, IntelliBeam Auto High Beams and more. 

 

Certain SUVs also have available safety and driver assistance features, such as Adaptive Cruise Control, Rear Camera Mirror, Enhanced Automatic Parking Assist and Driver Attention Assist.

Safety or driver assistance features are no substitute for the driver’s responsibility to operate the vehicle in a safe manner. Read the vehicle Owner’s Manual for important feature limitations and information.

What are the fuel economy ratings for Buick SUVs?

Buick SUV fuel economy varies between each model and depends on different factors such as cargo size.

2025 Envista EPA-est. MPG city/hwy: 28/32 (FWD)

2025 Encore GX EPA-est. MPG city/hwy: 29/31 (FWD),* 26/28 (AWD)

2025 Envision EPA-est. MPG city/hwy: 22/28 (AWD)

2025 Enclave EPA-est. MPG city/hwy: 20/27 (FWD), 19/24 (AWD)

Do Buick SUVs come with all-wheel drive?

2025 Envision comes standard with AWD. 2025 Encore GX and 2025 Enclave come standard with FWD and available AWD.

 

Available AWD sends power to the front and rear wheels to optimize traction on dry, wet or snow and ice-covered roads.
